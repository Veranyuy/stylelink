Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. Controller

StyleLink is responsible for the processing described in this policy. Questions: support@stylelink.app.

2. Data we collect

  • Account: name, email, phone number, role (client or provider), city, profile photo, and language preference.
  • Activity: bookings, selected services, appointment times, statuses, prices, reviews, favourites, and messages.
  • Provider details: business name, category, city/quarter, bio, photos, working hours, services, and prices.
  • Location: precise location only when you grant permission, used to record a provider's arrival. You can refuse; booking continues without GPS.
  • Notifications: Firebase device token and your notification preferences.
  • Diagnostics: technical data, error logs, and usage analytics (Firebase Analytics; Sentry when configured).

3. How we use data

  • Provide the service and manage bookings and messaging.
  • Send booking and account notifications you have enabled.
  • Keep the platform secure, prevent fraud, and enforce our Terms.
  • Understand usage and improve the App.
  • Meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only as needed with:

  • the provider for a booking, and the client on the provider side;
  • Supabase (authentication, database, and file storage);
  • Google Firebase (push notifications and analytics);
  • Sentry, when crash reporting is enabled;
  • Resend, for provider reminder emails;
  • authorities when required by law.

These providers may process data outside Cameroon under appropriate contractual safeguards.

5. Security

Traffic uses HTTPS/TLS. Access to data is limited by row-level security (RLS) policies and least-privilege roles. Verification codes are stored separately from booking rows and are not readable by providers. No system is perfectly secure; please do not send sensitive information in messages.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is retained while your account is active. Deleting your account removes the associated profile, bookings, messages, reviews, favourites, and stored images, subject to legal retention obligations and temporary technical backups.

7. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, or deletion of your data, object to certain processing, or complain to the competent authority. Contact support@stylelink.app.

8. Location and notifications

Location and notifications are optional. Refusing location does not prevent booking. You can disable notifications in the App or your device settings.

9. Children

StyleLink is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect children's data.

10. Cookies and local storage

The website uses technical storage required to run the App (session, preferences, and offline cache). We do not use advertising cookies.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always published here and in the App.
